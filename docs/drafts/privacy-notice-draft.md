## 2. What we collect

We collect only what each stage needs.

**Registration (all tracks)**

- Team name
- Member full names, nicknames, grade, and class (internal track) or school (external track)
- Member email addresses
- Representative LINE ID (optional)
- Support needs notes (optional). Please do not write medical records here.

**External track only**

- Copies of each member's national ID card or passport
- Each member's ปพ.7 (student status certificate)
- Supervisor name, role, school or organization, email, and phone (phone is optional)
- Payment details: payer name, amount, date, time, transaction reference, and the payment slip image or receipt link

**Round 1 submission**

- Project title and language
- Link to the team's Google Drive folder, which holds the video pitch, pitch deck and impact brief
- Video length

**Consent forms (Round 1 and Round 2)**

- Guardian name, relationship to the student, phone, and email
- Drawn signatures of the guardian and the student

**Finalists only (Round 2)**

- Event-day phone number
- Emergency contacts
- Dietary needs and accessibility needs
- Corrections to names on certificates

**Judging**

- Judges' scores and comments about the submitted work

**Technical**

- Security logs of actions on the website: time, action, and IP address (used to limit repeated requests)
